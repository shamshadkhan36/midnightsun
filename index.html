About Me & Studio Heritage

From A Mountain Ridge To The Global Screen.

Hailing from the serene valleys of Uttarakhand and refined across Mumbai’s film industry, Midnight Sun Studios was established by a passionate multi-disciplinary filmmaker. With rigorous professional training in Cinematography, VFX Pro, Editing, and Master Color Grading from leading institutions—including MAAC, Maharaja Agrasen College, SIFT (Mumbai), and ZIMA (Mumbai)—our approach bridges technical mastery with authentic human emotion.

Since founding our independent production banner in 2019, we have directed and produced critically acclaimed projects:

★ Parwaaz Ek Udaan – Best Trailer Winner, IFP 50-Hour Challenge
★ Glaani – Official Selection, Pune Short Film Festival
★ Mera Pahad Mera Himala – Zee Music Feature (8M+ Views)
★ Binsar Mahadev – Acclaimed Cultural Documentary
