Midnight Sun Studios

Born from a deep conviction that filmmaking should be unvarnished, socially rooted, and visually extraordinary. Combining specialized training from premier institutes—including MAAC, Maharaja Agrasen College, SIFT (Mumbai), and ZIMA (Mumbai)—we unite screenwriting and camera operation with master-level DaVinci Resolve color science.

Since founding our independent production house in 2019, our projects have garnered major festival recognition, including Parwaaz Ek Udaan (IFP 50-Hour Challenge Trailer Winner), Glaani (Pune Short Film Festival Selection), and the hit music video Mera Pahad Mera Himala on Zee Music (8M+ views).
